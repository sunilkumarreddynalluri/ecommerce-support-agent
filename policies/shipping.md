# Shipping Policy

Standard domestic shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs extra.

Free standard shipping applies to orders above $50.

International shipping is available to 40 countries and takes 7-14 business days. Customers are responsible for import duties and taxes.

Orders are processed within 1 business day. Tracking information is available once an order has shipped. If a package shows no tracking movement for 7 days, contact support so we can open an investigation with the carrier.
