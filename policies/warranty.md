# Warranty Policy

Electronics such as earbuds and chargers carry a 1 year warranty against manufacturing defects.

Apparel, footwear and bags carry a 90 day warranty against manufacturing defects such as broken stitching or sole separation.

The warranty does not cover water damage, accidental damage, normal wear and tear, or unauthorized repairs.

To make a warranty claim, contact support with your order ID and photos of the defect. Warranty claims are reviewed by the support team, which decides between repair, replacement or refund.
