# Returns and Refunds Policy

Change of mind: items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unused and in original packaging. A prepaid return label is provided. The refund covers the item price only.

Damaged or wrong items: if an item arrives damaged, defective or is not what was ordered, report it within 7 days of delivery for a full refund including shipping costs. Photos may be requested.

Final sale items: items marked "final sale" or "clearance" cannot be returned for change of mind. They can only be refunded if they arrive damaged or wrong, within 7 days of delivery.

Refund process: all refund requests are reviewed and approved by our support team within 2 business days. Approved refunds return to the original payment method in 5-7 business days.

Cancellations: orders that are still in "processing" status can be cancelled for a full refund. Orders that have already shipped cannot be cancelled and must be returned after delivery.
